## Game plan
- Token ping deck: face dilemmas, let Sisko make Officer tokens at your end step, and turn every token into damage with ETB pingers (Impact Tremors, Purphoros, Agate Instigator, Corpse Knight, Elim Garak).
- Max out the ping engine, dilemmas and Officer token production; fill the rest with cheap interaction.
- Elesh Norn and Roaming Throne double Sisko's trigger; Parallel Lives doubles the tokens. Panharmonicon doesn't double Sisko.

## Protecting the engine
- Prefer counterspells over dedicated board-wipe protection.
- Don't plan around death triggers or opponents' wipes.
- Sisko's tokens come from a trigger at the beginning of your end step, so the risky window is between facing dilemmas and the end step.

## Deckbuilding choices
- 36 lands: Sisko's activation usually gets its money's worth.
- Elim Garak over Obsidian Order Operative: cheaper damage engine (only downside: Garak isn't an Officer).
- Esper Sentinel and Sylvan Library stay; don't test them as cuts.
